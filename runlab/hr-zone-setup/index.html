RUNLAB · 02

HR ZONE
SETUP.

Calculate five heart-rate ranges for your running watch. Use your actual measurements and choose the same method used by your watch.

HOW TO FIND YOUR NUMBERS & SET YOUR WATCH

Resting HR: Use a reliable reading while genuinely rested or your watch's recent resting-heart-rate trend. Max HR: Use a credible maximum from activity data or supervised testing. An age formula is only a rough estimate; do not attempt a maximal test just to fill in this form.

Choose the matching method: Heart Rate Reserve (HRR / Karvonen) uses both values; % Max HR uses your maximum alone. The same percentage produces different BPM figures. These are general estimates, not individually measured lactate or ventilatory thresholds.

  • Apple Watch: Watch Settings → Workout → Heart Rate Zones → Manual. Apple generally lets you edit Zone 2–4 boundaries, with Zones 1 and 5 determined around them. You can also use the Watch app on iPhone. Apple's automatic zones may differ.
  • Garmin: Garmin Connect → Garmin Devices → your device → Heart Rate Zones. Choose the matching %HRR or %Max HR calculation basis if offered, then adjust BPM settings. Some devices support sport-specific zones; sync afterward. Do not confuse this with %LTHR.
  • Other devices: Check whether manual zones are supported and whether their basis is max HR, HRR or threshold HR.

Apple Watch instructions · Garmin instructions.

Your measured resting BPM. Only used in HRR calculations.
Your credible observed maximum BPM, not a goal.
Choose the calculation system used by your watch or training plan.

RUNLAB Health & Safety Disclaimer

These calculations are general estimates—not a diagnosis, medical advice or individual coaching. Seek qualified advice when needed, and stop activity if you experience concerning symptoms. Read full health and training guidance.
