Photography & Media Rights

Use of WRC event images, consent, credits and photo-removal requests.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Event photography

WRC publishes race and club photographs to document activities, recognise participants and support club communications. An individual's attendance at an event is not an unlimited authorisation to use their likeness as a commercial endorsement.

Consent and sensitive uses

WRC should rely on an appropriate lawful basis for publication and seek specific permissions where required, particularly for endorsements, sensitive contexts or identifiable children. A registration form may provide separate media preferences; its own terms govern those choices.

Requests and removal

To request a correction, image removal or discussion of usage rights, email wadadarunclub@gmail.com with the page link and a description. WRC will review the request and take appropriate action regarding WRC-controlled channels. Removing an image from WRC's website cannot guarantee removal from third-party reposts or search caches.

Ownership

Photographers and third-party rights holders retain applicable ownership. Public availability does not mean an image may be commercially copied. See Copyright & Content Use.
