Contact & Privacy Requests

Reach WRC about events, data privacy, photography, accessibility and website issues.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Contact Wadada Run Club

WRC operates in Jamaica. For website or club matters, contact wadadarunclub@gmail.com. Mention the page, activity and relevant details so the enquiry can be handled appropriately.

Common requests

  • Privacy: ask what data is held, request corrections or address unwanted processing.
  • Events and refunds: provide the event, participant name, date and a non-sensitive payment reference.
  • Photographs or copyright: provide the URL and a description of the affected image or work.
  • Accessibility and technical problems: share the page link and the problem encountered.

Keep communications secure

Do not email account passwords, full payment-card numbers, medical records or sensitive identification documents unless a secure method has specifically been agreed. WRC may ask for appropriate verification before disclosing personal records.
