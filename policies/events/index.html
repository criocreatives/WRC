Event Participation & Safety

Event eligibility, safety guidance and the relationship to existing waivers.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Event rules and eligibility

Read the individual event's description for location, start time, distance, terrain, inclusions and eligibility. The current RafJam event page states that it is for participants aged 18 and older. Other activities may set different conditions. Sending a form does not automatically secure a place.

Existing participant waiver

The RafJam registration form already includes a participation waiver. Participants must read and complete that form's waiver when registering. This policy supplements the official event materials, does not create a replacement waiver, and does not treat merely visiting this website as acceptance. No waiver removes rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

Safety responsibilities

  • Choose activities appropriate to your health, experience and fitness; obtain professional advice when needed.
  • Follow reasonable safety instructions, monitor weather and terrain, wear suitable clothing and equipment, bring water and communicate hazards.
  • Provide accurate requested safety information and emergency contacts with that person's knowledge.
  • Stop when unwell or injured and obtain medical help when appropriate. Do not assume medical cover, transport or an event bus will be present unless advertised.

Emergency response

Outdoor activities may involve traffic, falls, heat, severe weather or medical emergencies. Organisers may change routes, postpone activities or stop participation where necessary for reasonable safety reasons. Where needed, organisers may contact emergency services and an emergency contact supplied in the registration process.

Payment, cancellation and publicity

Payment requirements, inclusions and specific cancellation or refund conditions should be communicated before payment. See Refunds & Cancellations. Registration data is governed by Privacy, and photography inquiries by Media & Photography. Questions before joining: wadadarunclub@gmail.com.
