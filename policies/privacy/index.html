Privacy Policy

WRC's collection and use of website and event-registration information.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who this notice covers

Wadada Run Club (WRC) is responsible for the personal information it uses to administer its own website, communications and club activities. A partner or independent race organiser may process information separately. Contact us at wadadarunclub@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we may process

  • Event or club registrations: a participant's name, contact information, eligibility details, event selection and answers provided on the relevant form.
  • Safety-related information when a form requests it, including a participant's readiness responses and emergency contact details. Such information may be sensitive and requires particular care.
  • Enquiries, messages, race results and photographs in connection with WRC activities.
  • Basic page-visit statistics through LibreCounter. Its image request communicates website/referrer information and network metadata to the service.

Purposes and lawful handling

We use information to administer requested activities, contact registrants, plan logistics, communicate safety information, manage emergencies, respond to enquiries, publish appropriate event coverage and meet applicable obligations. The lawful basis varies by purpose: for example, an activity requested by a registrant, a legitimate operational interest, a legal duty, or consent where necessary. Sensitive information requires an appropriate additional lawful condition. An event form may contain separate consent or waiver choices.

External services and transfers

RafJam registration is conducted through Google Forms; responses are stored and handled through Google's services and made accessible to authorised organisers. GitHub Pages serves the website. The LibreCounter project describes cookie-free statistics without stored IP addresses; loading its tracking image nevertheless sends a request to that external service. These services may operate or process data outside Jamaica. Third-party races, Amazon and other linked sites have independent policies.

We do not sell participant registration data. WRC may share relevant details with authorised club organisers, service providers, partners supporting an activity, emergency responders when necessary, or others as required by law.

RUNLAB tools

The reviewed training, heart-rate and fuel calculators perform calculations in the browser; they do not require a WRC account or show an automatic submission of individual input values to WRC. Users may export results to their own devices. Do not send detailed medical records by ordinary email.

Security and retention

Access to event records should be limited to those who need the information for legitimate club purposes. Information must be stored securely and retained only for an appropriate period for the activity, safety follow-up, disputes and applicable law, then securely deleted or anonymised. The exact period depends on the record category and applicable obligations; contact us for the relevant retention details or to request deletion. Publishing this policy does not automatically delete old Google Form responses or saved records, which require an operational review.

Your rights

Under applicable Jamaican data-protection law, you may have rights to ask about, access and correct your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, or request erasure in appropriate circumstances. Send requests to wadadarunclub@gmail.com; we may need to verify identity before disclosing records. You may also seek guidance or make a complaint to the Office of the Information Commissioner. Browsing this site is not treated as blanket consent to activities that require consent.

Updates

We may revise this notice when our services or practices change. The revision date appears at the top.
