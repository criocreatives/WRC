Health, Training & Nutrition

Safety guidance for RUNLAB plans, HR-zone estimates and fueling tools.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

General information only

RUNLAB offers educational estimates; it does not diagnose or treat health conditions, prescribe clinical diet or medication, or substitute for a qualified doctor, sports dietitian or coach. Personal circumstances can make a suggestion inappropriate.

Training plans

An automatically generated programme is an illustration, not a promise of results or freedom from injury. Increase workload conservatively and account for prior fitness, fatigue, recovery, illness and environmental conditions. Seek professional guidance if uncertain.

Heart-rate zones

Heart-rate percentages, maximum-heart-rate formulas and wearable sensor readings are approximate and method-dependent. They are not diagnostic thresholds. Do not attempt an unsupervised maximal exercise test merely to supply calculator values.

Fuel and hydration

Energy, calorie, hydration and carbohydrate calculations are general estimates, not an individual dietary prescription. People with diabetes, cardiovascular or kidney conditions, pregnancy, eating disorders, relevant medications or other concerns should seek professional advice. Avoid severe calorie restriction or unfamiliar fuel on race day.

When to stop

Stop exercising when you experience chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness, confusion, severe pain or similar concerning symptoms, and seek appropriate urgent assistance. The website does not provide live monitoring, medical treatment or emergency response.

Privacy of entries

RUNLAB calculations reviewed appear to run on the user's device rather than submit measurements to WRC. See Privacy Policy for details.
