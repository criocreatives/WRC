Accessibility Statement

WRC's accessibility objectives and how to report issues with website features.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Our approach

WRC aims to make its website usable with assistive technologies, different screen sizes and keyboard navigation. It works toward recognised good practices for clear headings, legible contrast, image descriptions and operable forms. This statement does not claim formal WCAG certification.

Limitations

Older pages, photo carousels, images and linked third-party forms may contain accessibility barriers. WRC will review reported problems and prioritise reasonable corrections.

Report a barrier

Email wadadarunclub@gmail.com with the affected page URL and description of the problem. If helpful, explain what device or assistive technology you are using. You may request event information in a usable alternative format where practical.
