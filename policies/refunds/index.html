Refunds & Cancellations

How WRC communicates paid event conditions, cancellation and refund requests.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Before paying

WRC may promote free or paid club activities. The particular event's published or directly communicated terms determine its price, payment timing, included services, withdrawal deadline and refund conditions, subject to applicable law. Do not assume a universal no-refund condition or an unconditional refund. Ask WRC to clarify any missing terms before sending payment.

Participant requests

For a withdrawal, cancellation, transfer or refund enquiry, email wadadarunclub@gmail.com with the event name and date, participant name and a non-sensitive transaction reference. Please do not send account passwords, complete card numbers or unnecessary identity documents. Requests are reviewed in light of the event's communicated terms and legal obligations.

Changes or cancellation by organisers

If an activity is cancelled, postponed or materially changed by WRC, organisers will communicate relevant next steps and applicable payment options, which may include refunds, credits or transfers where offered or required by law. Outcomes may differ if third-party service costs have been incurred and lawfully disclosed.

Third-party races

External races and commercial events may have their own booking and refund policies. Their organisers and registration platforms control their transactions; WRC's website links do not make WRC the seller of every listed event.

RafJam Hike

The current hike page lists JMD $5,000 and states that a registration submission does not by itself prove payment or guarantee a place. Clarify cancellation and refund conditions with WRC before paying. Its existing form waiver is separate and unchanged.
