Cookies & Tracking

Current WRC visitor analytics and third-party cookie information.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

LibreCounter

WRC uses a LibreCounter image for basic traffic statistics. The LibreCounter developer states that the service does not use browser tracking cookies or store visitor IP addresses for statistics. Its image still generates a third-party network request and may transmit origin/referrer information. WRC has limited the referrer policy for that image to reduce full-path disclosures; this can reduce per-page statistics.

First-party technology

The current HTML pages reviewed do not deploy a WRC login system or identified first-party marketing cookies. Some RUNLAB functions use browser calculations and allow saving reports. This description will need updating if analytics, advertising, sign-in or embedded services change. No cookie acceptance pop-up is displayed solely for the currently documented cookie-free counter.

External websites

When following links to Google Forms, Google Calendar, Amazon and race-registration sites, those independent providers may use cookies or similar storage under their own privacy and cookie policies. WRC does not control their settings.

Controls and questions

You may restrict cookies and third-party requests using your browser controls; doing so may affect some features. Contact wadadarunclub@gmail.com with questions.
