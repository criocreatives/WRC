04 / INTERPRETATION

What the
data says.

Evidence-led observations, with uncertainty separated from what the screenshots actually measure.

01

Eight repetitions were tightly grouped compared with the outlier.

Excluding rep 8 (2:20), eight efforts average 1:47.4. Across those eight, recorded times span 1:40–1:52. The complete nine-rep average is 1:51.0.

02

Rep 8 was a temporary slowdown, not a poor finish.

You went from 2:20 on rep 8 to 1:40 on rep 9, a 40-second improvement over the same 400m distance. That rebound argues against interpreting the slowdown as a simple inability to run fast at the end. The reason is not established.

03

The recoveries became slower later.

The first two 100m recoveries each took 40 seconds. Recoveries 7 and 8 took 58 and 65 seconds. This suggests changing recovery behaviour under fatigue, but cannot separate intentional walking, traffic/turns, elevation or fatigue.

04

Substantial cardiovascular load.

Heart rate averaged 173 bpm, with 16:38 in Apple's Zone 4 and just 16 seconds in Zone 5. Heat and 91% humidity likely increased physiological stress; the assigned zones depend on the watch's configured thresholds.

05

VO₂ max is a watch estimate, not a laboratory result.

43.2 mL/kg/min is the value provided after this run. Its trend needs previous measurements under comparable conditions; this workout alone cannot establish whether VO₂ max increased.

06

Training prescription: consistency before absolute speed.

For a repeat of this workout, benchmark the eight non-outlier reps, aim for controlled, repeatable 400s, and standardize the 100m recoveries. The ninth-rep sprint demonstrates available finishing speed but does not define the best pace for every repetition.
